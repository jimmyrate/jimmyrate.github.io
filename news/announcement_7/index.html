Our multidisciplinary manuscript, “AI Identifies Compounds that Protect Retinal Ganglion Cells and Optic Nerve in Neurodegeneration by Alleviating ER Stress,” studies an AI-driven screening pipeline using ImageMol to identify neuroprotective candidates targeting endoplasmic reticulum stress, with potential relevance to glaucoma. Status update (October 2026): Nature Communications · Under review: major revision. This manuscript has not been accepted.
