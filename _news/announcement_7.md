🎉 Our work **CAML** (A Conflict-Aware Molecular Language Model Merging Framework for Multi-Constraint Molecular Generation) is accepted to [ACL 2026](https://2026.aclweb.org/) main conference! 🧬🤖. We introduce a novel game-theoretic framework that uses SACMA-ES to find the Nash Equilibrium among expert models, resolving property conflicts efficiently without re-training.
