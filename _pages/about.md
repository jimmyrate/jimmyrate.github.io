I am a Ph.D. candidate at the school of computer science, [Hunan University](https://www.hnu.edu.cn), under the supervision of Prof.[Xiangxiang Zeng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=B20HBMIAAAAJ) and Prof.[Yiping Liu](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=2eX_3XEAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). Previously, I obtained my M.E. degree at the school of computer science, [Hunan University](https://www.hnu.edu.cn) in 2022, working with Prof.[Lijun Cai](https://csee.hnu.edu.cn/people/cailijun) and Prof.[Xiangzheng Fu](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=YbU5MnsAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I earned my B.E. degree from Hengyang Normal University in 2019. 

***Research Interest***: My research focuses on AI-driven molecular and peptide discovery, with an emphasis on language models and multi-objective optimization.
- **Machine Learning**: 1) **Language Models**, studying how model parameters shape generated molecular properties; 2) **Multi-objective Optimization**, balancing competing objectives in molecular and peptide design.

***Publication & Activities***: My research has been published in interdisciplinary journals and computer science conferences, including [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51933-2), [Advanced Science](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/advs.202400829), [Bioinformatics](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/37/8/1060/5942971), [ACL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.896/), [AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/34092), [NeurIPS](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/c0ebffad509ae02bd60340690b1fdd5d-Abstract-Conference.html), and [CVPR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Liu_Sparse_Task_Vector_Mixup_with_Hypernetworks_for_Efficient_Knowledge_Transfer_CVPR_2026_paper.html). A full publication list can be found on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=AYPzTZsAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao).

Our **deepAMP** study identified the antimicrobial peptide **T2-9**, whose **in vitro activity against *E. coli* matched that of the clinical antibiotic levofloxacin** (MIC: **2.93 μg/mL for both**). The designed peptides are covered by a granted Chinese invention patent arising from collaborative research.

Our **HydrogelFinder** study identified **9 hydrogel-forming peptides among 17 synthesized candidates (52.9%)**, completing the design-to-experiment workflow in **19 days**. A HydrogelFinder-designed gel, **PD-DP-008 (JIJ02)**, has **entered Phase I clinical evaluation** (CTR20250456).

I also serve as a reviewer for:
- Conferences: ICLR (2024-2027), NeurIPS (2025-2026), AAAI (2026-2027)
- Journals: Bioinformatics, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Scientific Reports.

***More Things***: Honored to be recognized as a **Top Reviewer at NeurIPS 2026**.
